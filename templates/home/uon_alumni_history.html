About UONAA

History.

Two decades of a formally registered Alumni Association, from its founding in 2004 to its public launch on 5 February 2005.

01
In brief

In September 2004, the University of Nairobi Council asked Dr. Betty Gikonyo, an alumna and Council member, to chair a sub-committee on launching an alumni association. The University Council repealed Statute XXVIII (which had governed the old Convocation) to make way for it, and on Saturday, 5 February 2005, the Association was officially launched at a ceremony at the Chancellor's Court, presided over by the then Minister for Education, the late Prof. George Saitoti.

Twenty years on, the Association operates through a Secretariat at Kolobot Drive, an elected Executive Committee, and chapters across disciplines and the diaspora. Its programming spans mentorship, scholarships, and the public events that bring the alumni community together year on year.

The time-line below records the Association's verified milestones, drawn from its own newsletters, annual reports, and university records.

2004
Founding

The University Council acts

The Council repeals Statute XXVIII, which had governed the old Convocation, and asks Dr. Betty Gikonyo to chair a sub-committee on launching a proper Alumni Association. Prof. David Wasawo leads the Taskforce on Alumni Relations that drafts its blueprint.

2005
Launch

UONAA is officially launched

Launched 5 February 2005 at the Chancellor's Court, presided over by the then Minister for Education, the late Prof. George Saitoti. By year's end: 2,147 paid-up members, the first issue of "The Alumnus" newsletter, and the first disbursement to 36 students.

2007

Medical Chapter launched

The first of several discipline-specific alumni chapters, bringing together graduates from the College of Health Sciences.

2008

Three African leaders honoured

H.E. Mwai Kibaki, Rt. Hon. Raila Odinga, and H.E. Dr. Kofi Annan receive honorary degrees for their role in ending the violence that followed the 2007 election — the Association welcomes all three as alumni.

2014

Prof. David Wasawo passes away

The founding patron and intellectual father of UONAA dies on 4 February 2014, aged 91.

2015
10th Anniversary

Tenth anniversary, and the Education Chapter

A Gala Dinner at the Great Court on 6 February marks ten years since launch. The Education Chapter is launched on 30 April.

2016

Leadership transition

After eleven years as founding Chairperson, Dr. Betty Gikonyo hands over the leadership to Mr. Isaac Awuondo.

2017

School of Law Chapter launched

The Association's fifteenth chapter. The same year, KSh 2 million is donated to support needy and bright students.

2020

UONAA Scholarship Fund launched

Officially launched 13 March 2020 at the Vice-Chancellor's Parlour, with 52 students in the inaugural cohort — the Association's most ambitious scholarship commitment to date.

2025
20th Anniversary

Twenty years of UONAA

Celebrated 28 February 2025 under the theme "Let Us Embrace a Culture of Giving Back," honouring founding visionaries Dr. Joseph Barrage Wanjui, Prof. George Magoha, and Prof. David Wasawo.

03
Founding figures
Founding Patron
Prof. David Wasawo (1923 – 2014)

Led the 2003 Taskforce on Alumni Relations that produced the Association's blueprint and chaired its founding Interim Committee. Declined to stand for election as chairperson due to age, but remained a patron until his death in 2014.

Founding Chairperson
Dr. Betty Gikonyo

A consultant cardiologist and alumna who chaired the launch sub-committee and served as the Association's first Chairperson from 2005 to 2015, before handing over to Mr. Isaac Awuondo.

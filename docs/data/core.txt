
Our Mission and Vision
UONAA's mission is to empower, connect, and engage the alumni of the University of Nairobi, fostering a culture of excellence, service and innovation. We are committed to mobilizing resources, building strategic partnerships and networks, increasing memberships and strengthening student- alumni relations. Our vision is to be an influential Alumni Association that is engaged and driven by a shared commitment to excellence and service.


FOUNDATIONS OF UONAA
The strategic plan sets UONAA priorities for the next five years towards the vision. This strategic plan is purposefully built from the following tenets:
Motto
Our Alumni Our Strength!
Vision
To be an influential Alumni Association that is engaged and driven by a shared commitment to excellence and service.
Mission
To empower, connect, and engage the alumni of the University of Nairobi, fostering a culture of excellence, service and innovation. We are committed to mobilizing resources, building strategic partnerships and networks, increasing memberships and strengthening student- alumni relations.

Core Values
The UONAA has core values that reflect the principles and ideals that guide her as she works towards achieving her vision and mission as outlined in the strategic plan. These include: Integrity, accountability, transparency, loyalty, professionalism, excellence, care, innovation, service, Legacy, and environmental stewardship.
1.Integrity and Professionalism: Integrity is the foundation of our Association. We conduct ourselves with honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct in all interactions, ensuring the trust and credibility of the UONAA.
2. Collaboration: We recognize the power of collaboration. We build strong partnerships and networks with alumni, educational institutions, government agencies, and organizations to achieve our shared goals and advance the interests of our members.
3. Environmental Stewardship: We are committed to responsible environmental management. We value sustainability and actively promote eco-friendly practices in all our activities, events, and operations. We aim to minimize our carbon footprint, reduce waste, conserve resources, and foster environmental awareness among our alumni and partners, contributing to a greener and more sustainable future.
4. Excellence: We are committed to upholding the highest standards of excellence in all our activities, programs, and services, reflecting the prestige of our alma mater, the University of Nairobi.
5. Inclusivity: We celebrate the rich diversity of our alumni community and are dedicated to fostering an inclusive environment where every alumnus, regardless of background or affiliation, feels valued and welcomed.
6. Innovation: We embrace innovation and creativity to adapt to evolving needs and challenges. We seek innovative solutions to enhance alumni engagement and contribute positively to society.

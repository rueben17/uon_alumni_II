# University of Nairobi Alumni Association (UONAA): Articles, Governance, and Structure

## 📜 Official Documents and Structural Framework

The University of Nairobi Alumni Association (UONAA) is governed by a detailed constitution and operates under specific guidelines.

### 1. The Official Launch and Succession

- **Launch Date**: The UONAA was officially launched in **February 2005**.
- **Legal Succession**: The UONAA is the legal successor to the **Convocation**, which was previously established under Statute XXVIII of the University of Nairobi. This statute was repealed to give birth to the Alumni Association.
- **Acknowledgment**: A 2012 speech by the Chairperson confirms that the Association was born from the Council's 2004 decision and the subsequent repealing of the Convocation statute.

### 2. The Constitution (2019 Version)

The official constitution outlines the core rules:

#### Membership Categories

- **Founder Members**: The first **5,000** persons to register.
- **Full Members**: All graduates of the University, honorary degree holders, permanent staff, and University Council members.
- **Honorary Members**: Individuals who have had a sustained impact on alumni or the University community.
- **Life Members**: Any member who pays the prescribed life membership fee.

#### Membership Fees

- **Annual Membership**: Kshs. 2,000 per year.
- **Bronze Life Member**: Kshs. 25,000 (one-time).
- **Silver Life Member**: Kshs. 50,000 (one-time).
- **Gold Life Member**: Kshs. 100,000 (one-time).
- **Corporate Member**: Kshs. 1,000,000.

### 3. Governance Structure

The draft constitution details a formal structure:

- **Executive Committee**: Elected by members at the Annual General Meeting (AGM) for a **three-year term**. It includes positions such as Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, Treasurer, Secretary, and Organizing Secretary.
- **Board of Trustees**: Provides advisory services to the Executive Committee.
- **Board of Management**: An administrative body that fixes membership dues and manages the Association's affairs.

### 4. Alumni Chapters

The Association is decentralized into chapters, with formal guidelines for their formation and operation:

- **Formation**: A College, School, or Institute can form a chapter by recruiting at least **ten (10) Life Members**.
- **Roles**: Chapters are responsible for fundraising, recruitment, organizing reunions, and developing mentorship programs with current students.
- **Funding**: Chapter-level funds are placed in a designated "project account" and are used for student scholarships, research, and specific University projects.

## 📈 Key Milestones and Current Activities

The Association has been active in several initiatives since its founding:

- **Milestone Event (2009)**: A major Alumni Reunion and "Elders' Night" was held at the KICC for the Class of '09, with the Vice-President as the Chief Guest. This event also saw the launch of a social networking platform for alumni and the initiation of a project for an Alumni Centre with **Kshs. 10 million** in seed money.
- **Automatic Membership**: By 2009, the Chairperson announced that graduating students became **automatic members** of UONAA through their convocation fee, which was a significant step in expanding the base.
- **Current Benefits**: Members today enjoy various benefits, including a **Membership Smart Card** for access to services like the Library, discounts at the University bookstore, networking opportunities, and participation in University governance.
- **How to Join**: Registration can now be completed online, with payment accepted via **M-PESA Paybill Number 826300**.

## Summary

The UONAA's origins lie in the University of Nairobi Council's 2004 decision to replace a dormant Convocation with a vibrant alumni association. The 2005 remittance of the Kshs. 1,000 Alumni Fee gave the Association its initial financial base. Over the following two decades, the Association developed a formal constitution, membership tiers, governance organs, chapter system, and alumni services. By 2026, it stands as a structured, self-sustaining network supporting mentorship, fundraising, scholarships, University governance, and institutional development.
